Our Business Verticals

Driving Impact Across Key Sectors

Aquiline Crystal Nigeria Limited operates across three strategic verticals — Infrastructure & Real Estate, Industrial Commodity Trading, and Agribusiness & Agro-Industrial Development — each structured for institutional-grade returns and measurable national impact.

Build-Operate-Transfer

Infrastructure & Real Estate Development

AQCNL develops institutional-grade infrastructure across Nigeria's education and housing sectors, using Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT) structures that align investor returns with long-term national development impact.

Our current client is Evangel University, Akaeze, where we hold a secured Approval-in-Principle (AIP) for a large-scale student hostel development. Our long-term vision is to build over 20,000 hostel rooms across multiple institutions nationwide.

Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT) Student Housing

Nigeria's tertiary institutions face a severe shortage of quality student accommodation. AQCNL addresses this gap through BOT hostel developments,financing,constructing,and operating modern student housing under long-term concession agreements with universities,before eventual transfer of ownership at concession maturity.

Our BOT model is built for institutional investment: predictable rental income, long concession tenors, and structured exit windows for multiple investor classes.

Current Client: Evangel University

Our active infrastructure client is Evangel University, Akaeze, Ebonyi State, where AQCNL has secured an Approval-in-Principle (AIP) for a large-scale student hostel development under a long-term BOT concession. This project is structured with disciplined capex and rental assumptions for DFI-grade underwriting, and serves as the anchor project for our broader national infrastructure vision.

Our Vision: 20,000+ Beds Nationwide

Beyond Evangel, AQCNL's infrastructure strategy is built as a scalable, replicable platform rather than a single-project exercise. Our vision is to develop over 20,000 student hostel rooms across multiple Nigerian tertiary institutions, using a standardized structuring, financial modelling, and governance methodology at each site. This positions AQCNL to engage further institutions and investors as the model proves out at Evangel.

Investment Structure & Institutional Partners

Our infrastructure developments are structured to meet the underwriting requirements of diverse investor classes, including:

  • Pension Fund Administrators (PFAs) — long-duration, infrastructure-matched fixed income exposure
  • Development Finance Institutions (DFIs) — including relationships developed through Nordrock Limited and FMO
  • Private Equity & Venture Capital
  • High-Net-Worth Individuals and Diaspora Investors
  • Crowdfunding platforms for accessible retail participation in Nigerian infrastructure

We also work with strategic partners such as Trinity Telecommunication (Hubli, Karnataka, India) to bring international best practice and technology to our developments.

Partner With Us We welcome conversations with universities, investors, and development finance institutions interested in our BOT hostel model.
Specialized Trading Desk

Industrial Commodity Trading

AQCNL operates a specialized industrial commodity trading desk serving Nigeria's fertilizer, petrochemical, mining, and manufacturing sectors. We connect verified international suppliers with Nigeria's largest industrial buyers, managing sourcing, logistics, quality assurance, and contract execution from origin to delivery.

Commodities We Trade

  • Granular Sulphur— High-purity granular sulphur (99.8% purity, Iraqi origin) supplied to fertilizer producers and industrial chemical manufacturers across Nigeria.
  • Sulphuric Acid— Bulk industrial-grade sulphuric acid for fertilizer blending, mineral processing, and chemical manufacturing applications.
  • Calcined Petroleum Coke (CPC)— Sourced for aluminium smelting, steel production, and other carbon-intensive industrial processes, including emerging Nigeria-origin supply chains linked to domestic refining capacity.
  • Fumigation Chemicals— Regulated agricultural and industrial fumigation products supplied in compliance with Nigerian safety and handling standards.
  • Carbon Monoxide— Industrial gas supply for specialized manufacturing and processing applications.
  • Monazite Sand— Rare earth mineral sand trading, conducted under full NNRA (Nigerian Nuclear Regulatory Authority) licensing and compliance protocols.

Supply Chain & Trade Terms

AQCNL structures commodity transactions on internationally recognized trade terms to protect all parties:

  • CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight) pricing structures
  • MT 700 Irrevocable Letters of Credit for secured payment
  • Independent quality verification via SGS and Intertek pre-shipment inspection
  • End-to-end logistics coordination from port of origin to Nigerian point of delivery

Key Markets & Buyer Relationships

Our commodity trading desk maintains active engagement with Nigeria's leading industrial consumers, including major fertilizer producers, petrochemical processors, and chemical manufacturers. We source both from established international suppliers and are actively developing Nigeria-origin supply chains — including potential calcined petroleum coke offtake linked to domestic refining output — to strengthen local supply chain resilience and reduce import dependency.

Sourcing & Origin Assurance

Every commodity transaction AQCNL facilitates is backed by verified origin documentation, regulatory compliance (including NNRA licensing where applicable), and independent inspection at load port and discharge. This rigor protects buyers from quality risk and ensures reliable, repeatable supply relationships.

Public-Private Partnerships

Agribusiness & Agro-Industrial Development

Aquiline Crystal Nigeria Limited (AQCNL) builds and operationalizes bankable agribusiness ventures that strengthen Nigeria's food security, create rural employment, and deliver strong, verifiable returns for institutional investors.

Our agribusiness practice is anchored by the Abia Commercial Mushroom Enterprise (ACME) a public-private partnership currently in advanced-stage discussion with Abia State Government — and we are actively open to structuring similar partnerships with other Nigerian states.

The Abia Commercial Mushroom Enterprise (ACME)

ACME is our flagship agribusiness initiative: a large-scale commercial mushroom cultivation enterprise being developed in partnership with the Abia State Government, currently at an advanced stage of engagement.

ACME is designed to cultivate high-value mushroom species including Oyster, Shiitake, Reishi, and Lion's Mane for domestic and export markets, using controlled-environment greenhouse production and solar-powered infrastructure. The project is structured as a demonstrable, replicable model for state-level agribusiness PPPs.

Beyond Abia, AQCNL is open to further discussions with other Nigerian state governments interested in adopting a similar public-private partnership approach to commercial agriculture.

Key Features of Our Mushroom Agribusiness Model:

  • Multi-species cultivation (Oyster, Shiitake, Reishi, Lion's Mane, King Oyster, White Button)
  • Solar-powered greenhouse infrastructure for climate-controlled yield consistency
  • Structured off-take and storefront distribution to institutional, retail, and export buyers
  • Phase 2 diversification into functional mushroom products (immunity and wellness-focused derivatives)
  • Investment-grade financial modelling aligned to DFI evaluation standards

Public-Private Partnerships in Agriculture

AQCNL structures and negotiates agribusiness PPPs directly with state governments, positioning agriculture as a driver of internally generated revenue (IGR) and rural economic development. Our PPP model protects both public interest and investor capital through clear equity structures, anti-expropriation provisions, and transparent governance.

While Abia State represents our current and most advanced state partnership, our model is built to scale. We welcome conversations with any Nigerian state government interested in exploring a commercial agribusiness PPP for their region.

Technical & Financial Partnerships

We work alongside recognized institutional partners to ensure our agribusiness ventures meet international bankability standards:

  • NIHORT (National Institute of Horticultural Research) technical partner for cultivation science, species selection, and agronomic best practice
  • Nordrock Limited (Belgium) financial consultant and capital introduction partner for DFI-grade transactions
  • FMO (Dutch Entrepreneurial Development Bank) target development finance institution for agribusiness capital raises
  • NIRSAL (Nigeria Incentive-Based Risk Sharing System for Agricultural Lending) strategic partner for strengthening PPP bankability and agricultural finance structuring, alongside engagement with other Nigerian agricultural finance institutions to expand access to concessionary capital
